The process

How it works

1Design

Choose a product, fabric, colour and sizes and place your logo or text.

2Request a quote

Add one or more products and send your request. No payment needed.

3We check and quote

We check your artwork and options and reply by e-mail with a quote.

4Production

After your approval we produce your order in Bangkok and ship it to you.

What is the minimum order?

It depends on the product and decoration. Tell us your quantities and we include the possibilities in our quote.

Which logo file is best?

A vector file (AI, EPS, SVG or PDF) gives the sharpest print and embroidery. A large PNG works fine for the preview and to get started.

Is the preview exact?

The preview shows placement and proportions. Colours and sizes are checked by our team before production; we send you a proof or sample if you ask for it.

Can you make my own private-label brand?

Yes. Besides teamwear and merchandise, Eleven Fashion helps brands with design, fabric sourcing and production. Mention it in your request.
