01
From Code to Commerce

It started with a line of code.

At Central Point, I led development teams building e-commerce platforms before anyone called it omnichannel. At Decathlon, I redesigned the entire digital-to-store experience for the Netherlands.

The code was never the point. Understanding how people buy — that was the point.

02
Marketing Meets Technology

Where data meets instinct.

At Saturn and MediaMarkt, I bridged front-end development with marketing — building campaigns that converted because they were built on real user behavior, not assumptions.

Then at WP Masters, I ran both departments as one. Marketing without tech is guessing. Tech without marketing is invisible.

03
Building from Scratch

Eight stores. One vision.

In 2019, I founded The Health House. No investors. No playbook. Just a relentless belief that sports nutrition deserved better retail — better stores, better advice, better brands.

Today: 8 physical stores, a growing webshop, a wholesale operation serving gyms and studios nationwide, and a white-label division helping brands launch their own lines.

"Retail is not dying. Bad retail is dying. The brands that win are the ones that show up — in real places, with real people, offering real value."
Ismael Khalladi
