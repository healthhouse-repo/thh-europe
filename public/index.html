Frequently asked questions

What is the minimum order?

It depends on the product and the decoration. Tell us how many pieces you need and we include the possibilities in our quote.

Which file should I upload for my logo?

A vector file (AI, EPS, SVG or PDF) gives the best result for printing and embroidery. A high-resolution PNG also works to get started.

Can I get a sample first?

Tick "Pre-production sample" under extras and we include it in the quote.

Do you also make private-label clothing?

Yes, besides teamwear and merchandise Eleven Fashion helps brands with design, fabric sourcing and production. Mention it in your request.
