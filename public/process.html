How it works

Step by step

From design to delivery

  1. 01Design

    You design your product online or send us your idea, artwork and quantities. We check everything and send you a quote.

  2. 02Mockup approval

    We prepare a digital mockup with exact placement, colours and sizes. You approve it in your account or ask for changes.

  3. 03In progress

    After approval and payment we source the fabric, cut, sew, print or embroider and check the quality.

  4. 04In transit

    Your order is packed and shipped worldwide. You find the carrier and tracking number in your account.

  5. 05Delivered

    Your products arrive. Reorders are simple: the design and specs stay in your account.

What is gsm?

Grams per square metre of fabric. The higher the number, the thicker and heavier the fabric.

Lightweight

Cool and breathable. Good for hot climates, events, promo and sportswear. Lower price and shipping cost.

Midweight

The all-round standard: soft, not see-through and great for printing. Example: a 300 gsm hoodie.

Heavyweight

Thick, durable and structured with a premium, boxy look. Popular for merch and streetwear.

The fabric type matters too: French terry is looped on the inside and breathable, brushed fleece is soft and warmer, loopback is a heavy terry for a premium feel. In the designer you choose the exact weight and fabric type, or ask us to advise.

FAQ

Questions

What is the minimum order?

It depends on the product and decoration. Tell us your quantities and we include the possibilities in our quote.

Which logo file is best?

A vector file (AI, EPS, SVG or PDF) gives the sharpest print and embroidery. A large PNG works fine for the preview and to get started.

Is the preview exact?

The preview shows placement and proportions. Colours and sizes are checked by our team before production; we send you a proof or sample if you ask for it.

Can you make my own private-label brand?

Yes. Besides teamwear and merchandise, Eleven Fashion helps brands with design, fabric sourcing and production. Mention it in your request.

Can I follow my order?

Yes. Log in to your account with your e-mail address. You see every step: design, mockup approval, production, shipping with tracking and delivery.

Which payment methods do you accept?

Credit and debit cards, Apple Pay and Google Pay worldwide, PromptPay, TrueMoney, LINE Pay, Alipay and WeChat Pay in Asia, iDEAL, Bancontact and SEPA in Europe, and international bank transfer.

Do you ship to my country?

We ship worldwide. Shipping costs and delivery time depend on the destination and the size of your order and are included in your quote.

When do I pay?

The payment terms are in your quote and on your invoice. You pay online in your account or by bank transfer.
