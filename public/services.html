What we do

One stop shop

Everything you need to make your own clothing, under one roof in Bangkok.

Services

From idea to delivery

Work with us on one step or on the whole route. Tell us what you need and we put together a proposal that fits your quantities, budget and timing.

Fashion design

From a rough idea to a finished tech pack. We help with design, fit, size charts and samples so your product is ready for production.

Fabric sourcing

Cotton, organic cotton, blends, fleece and performance fabrics in the weight and colour you need, including custom Pantone dyeing.

Manufacturing

Cutting, sewing and finishing in Bangkok with quality checks at every step, for small runs and larger series.

Printing & embroidery

Screen print, embroidery, DTF, heat transfer, sublimation and woven labels or patches.

Teamwear

Kits, training wear and fan merchandise for gyms, clubs, teams and events, with names and numbers if needed.

Merchandise

T-shirts, hoodies, caps and bags with your logo for companies, artists and brands.

Private label

Your own clothing brand: custom neck labels, hang tags, polybags and packaging with your branding.

Distribution

Packing per size or per person and shipping to any country in the world, with tracking.
