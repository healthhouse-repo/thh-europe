About us

We are Eleven Fashion

A clothing manufacturer and wholesaler in Bangkok, working for customers all over the world.

Made in
Bangkok

Eleven Fashion helps brands, teams and companies make their own clothing: from the first idea to the finished, packed product.

We combine design, fabric sourcing, manufacturing, printing, embroidery and distribution. That means one contact person, one plan and one quality standard for your whole order.

This online portal makes working with us simple. Design your product, request a quote and follow your order step by step: mockup, production, shipping and delivery. Your invoices and payments are in the same place.
